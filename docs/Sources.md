# Sources

Every map and table Real Worlds is made from. The maps are published by NASA, NOAA and the USGS (most are works of the
United States government; the rest were released by NASA's missions through the Planetary Data System) for anyone to
use with credit; each is credited here. The textures in the download were made from them by the scripts in
`tools/realworlds` (see [Building it yourself](Building.md)).

## Figures

* **Sizes, gravity, spins:** JPL's astrodynamic parameters (DE440 GM values), planetary and satellite physical
  parameters; the Sun's radius the IAU 2015 nominal 695,700 km; Ceres from Dawn (Konopliv et al. 2018).
* **Prime meridians:** the IAU WGCCRE 2015 rotation elements.
* **Orbits:** JPL's "Approximate Positions of the Planets" (Table 1, J2000 ecliptic, 1800-2050) and the planetary
  satellites' mean orbital elements with each Laplace plane's pole; the obliquity of the ecliptic at J2000.
* **Atmospheres:** Earth, the U.S. Standard Atmosphere 1976 (COESA); Venus, VIRA (Seiff et al. 1985) to 100 km;
  Mars, the Viking 1 and 2 entry profiles (Seiff & Kirk 1977, NASA PDS atmospheres node); Titan, Huygens HASI
  (Fulchignoni et al. 2005); Jupiter, the Galileo probe; Saturn, Uranus and Neptune, Voyager's radio occultations
  and the published reviews; molar masses from NASA's planetary fact sheets and the probes' measured helium.
* **Earth's sky (with Scatterer):** Bruneton & Neyret (2008, 2017): Rayleigh 5.802, 13.558, 33.1 per thousand km at
  680, 550 and 440 nm, scale height 8 km; ozone round 25 km.
* **Saturn's rings:** NASA's Saturnian rings fact sheet (the D, C, B, A and F rings, the Cassini Division, the Encke
  and Keeler gaps) and Cassini's occultations for their optical depths.
* **Biome names:** the IAU's gazetteer of planetary nomenclature (the maria, Olympus Mons, Valles Marineris,
  Hellas and Argyre Planitiae, Caloris, Maxwell Montes, Occator, Sputnik Planitia and the rest).
