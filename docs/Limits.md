# Limits

What Real Worlds 0.1 does not do yet.

* **No tilted worlds.** KSP spins every world about one axis; here Earth's. So every world turns about Earth's pole and
  orbits are laid out against Earth's equator: Earth's seasons are right, but Mars, Saturn and Uranus do not lean
  their own way (Uranus does not roll on its side).
* **Launch sites.** Making History's sites and the island airfield are taken out, not moved to real ones (they stood on
  Kerbin's continents). Moving them to Woomera, Baikonur or Edwards needs their ground made flat where they would stand.
* **Earth's fine detail.** Under its 4.9 km height map Earth's ground is smooth slopes; the other measured worlds have a
  light noise below their maps' pixels, but on Earth the game's noise would raise its low coasts out of the sea.
* **Drawn, not mapped:** the bands of Saturn, Uranus and Neptune (no global map of their cloud tops is published),
  Venus's cloud tops and its ground's colour, Titan's haze, Saturn's rings (from their measured radii and opacities).
* **Made-up relief** on the worlds no spacecraft has measured all over (the moons of Jupiter and Saturn, Ceres, Triton,
  Pluto and Charon, Phobos and Deimos): noise at each world's real range of heights, with the real maps' colours.
* **Skies** are likenesses: Scatterer cannot model Venus's cloud deck or Titan's layered haze; their colours were set by
  eye against photographs.
* **Unseen ground.** Where no spacecraft looked (Triton's north, Pluto's and Charon's south, smaller gaps elsewhere), the
  map is a plain cap of the world's mean colour, softened into what was seen.
* **From high over land, a bluish veil.** From 30 to 150 km up, Earth's land looks veiled in blue-purple; Scatterer's
  scattering and the game's own terrain fog were each switched off and were not the cause. Not found yet. (From further
  off, Scatterer's settings for Kerbin took the whole Earth for sea, the map marking none: they washed it blue and made
  the sun glint on Africa. They are off for Earth now.)
* **No sun glint on Earth's seas from afar:** the map does not yet mark which of it is sea.
* **Titan's haze** is a third as thick as first set, for its look from space (it came out a dim brown ball); its sky from
  the ground has not been looked at again since.
* **Natural Weather** draws Earth's kind of cloud on Titan, Venus and Mars too (it was made for Kerbin's worlds, whose
  places these take); its clouds over Earth from orbit are the fields of its 0.4.0.
* **Rockets.** Low Earth orbit takes about 9.4 km/s; the stock parts were made for Kerbin. Real Worlds changes no part.
* **Windows and Linux** have not been run. On Windows, KSPTextureLoader uploads textures its own (working) way, so Real
  Worlds' fix for the Mac does nothing there.
