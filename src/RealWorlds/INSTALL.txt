Real Worlds @VERSION@
for Kerbal Space Program 1.12.x

The real Solar System at its real size, from NASA's and JPL's figures and maps: 22 worlds
at their measured sizes, masses, spins and orbits, their ground from spacecraft maps, their
air from measured profiles, biomes from the maps, real dates and faster time warp.

IT REPLACES EVERY WORLD
  Start a new save for it. Craft in a save made without it were made for Kerbin's worlds
  and may be lost (an orbit 100 km above Kerbin lies deep inside Earth). If you load one,
  Real Worlds keeps a copy of it as it was beside it, and says where.

INSTALL
  This mod needs Keystone and Kopernicus, and what Kopernicus needs (ModularFlightIntegrator,
  KSPTextureLoader and Harmony), which are separate downloads:
    https://spacedock.info/mod/4665/Keystone
    https://github.com/Kopernicus/Kopernicus/releases (it comes with the others it needs)
  Copy their folders into your KSP's GameData folder, and the RealWorlds folder from this
  download beside them, and start the game. To remove it, delete the RealWorlds folder (and
  go back to saves made without it).

USE
  Reaching low Earth orbit takes about 9.4 km/s: the stock rockets were made for Kerbin, so
  a rocket for Earth is far larger, or comes from a parts mod. The clock shows the real date
  (UTC) and time warp goes to 10,000,000 times; both can be switched off in the Keystone
  window: the keystone button on the game's toolbar, or Option-K on a Mac and Alt-K elsewhere.
  Where Scatterer is installed, its skies are set for the real worlds; EVE's stock clouds, made
  for Kerbin's worlds, are taken off them.

WHERE IT WORKS
  Made and tried on a Mac. On Windows it is the same configs and code, but nobody has run it
  there yet. Nobody has run it on Linux yet.

The maps it is made from are NASA's, NOAA's and the USGS's, free to use with credit; each
is credited on the mod's pages.

Everything else: https://github.com/IshiakiZ/ksp-real-worlds
